FAQ

Answers to the most frequently asked questions.

How secure is the data I share with Phonely AI?

Phonely is built with data security and compliance as core priorities. We support major industry standards and frameworks, including SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR, to help businesses protect sensitive customer information across every interaction.

That includes communications handled through phone calls, SMS, email, and other channels. Phonely uses secure infrastructure, encryption, access controls, and strong data handling practices to help keep customer data protected.

We understand that business communications are mission-critical. That is why security, reliability, and compliance are built into the foundation of the Phonely platform.

What languages does Phonely AI support?

Phonely AI supports 100+ languages across voice, SMS, chat, and more, with the ability to tailor experiences for regional accents, localized phrasing, and market-specific workflows.

How fast can Phonely set up my AI phone call assistant?

Phonely is designed for fast setup, with 70% of businesses getting started in less than 5 minutes. That means many teams can launch an AI phone call assistant quickly without a long implementation process.

For enterprise and large organizations, setup often includes more advanced workflows, integrations, and customization. In those cases, Phonely provides a dedicated team to help configure, deploy, and optimize your AI voice agent for your business needs and scale.

What industries can benefit from Phonely?

Phonely can support businesses across a wide range of industries that rely on phone calls, messaging, and high-volume customer conversations. Common use cases include healthcare, home services, legal, insurance, real estate, hospitality, e-commerce, financial services, automotive, education, and contact centers.

Any business that needs to handle customer support, appointment scheduling, lead qualification, routing, payments, follow-up, or outbound outreach can benefit from Phonely. The platform is especially valuable for teams that want to improve response times, increase call coverage, and deliver consistent customer experiences at scale.

Does Phonely let me create custom AI phone assistant workflows?

Yes. Phonely lets you customize your AI phone assistant around your business, including conversation flows, call scripts, tone of voice, routing logic, qualification questions, booking steps, payment workflows, and escalation paths. That means your assistant can follow your processes while sounding and responding the way you want.

For more advanced use cases, Phonely also supports custom workflows, integrations, and ongoing optimization, so your AI phone assistant can improve over time as your needs evolve.
