Marlow Home

Your bag

Demo panel

A concept for a Product Designer (E-Commerce and Motion) role. Marlow Home is invented, the products are samples, and nothing is charged, sent or stored outside this browser.

Try this (about 3 minutes)

  1. Filter by Living, then sort by price. Watch the cards slide to their new places.
  2. Open Linen Throw. Switch to Clay (only 2 left) and add it to your bag.
  3. Open Terracotta Planter. Size M is sold out, so try Notify me.
  4. In the bag, change a quantity, remove an item and press Undo. Try a wrong promo code, then WELCOME10.
  5. Check out. Choose Pick up in store to see each store’s stock. Pay with UPI.
  6. Edge cases: type fail@upi as the UPI ID, or use the buttons below.

Motion

Reduce motion

Following your device setting.

All motion here is built in code with CSS transitions, CSS keyframes and the Web Animations API. It was not made with Smart Animate, ProtoPie or Lottie. When reduced motion is on, movement becomes a fade or an instant change.

Durations
fast 120ms, base 200ms, slow 320ms
Easing 1
Standard cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1) for state changes
Easing 2
Emphasized cubic-bezier(.05,.7,.1,1) for things arriving

Why things move

10 motions. This is every animation in the prototype.
MotionWhat it doesWhy it existsDuration
Grid re-flowWhen you filter or sort, cards that stay slide to their new spot. New cards fade and settle in.Shows what changed, so you don’t have to re-scan the whole grid.320ms
Selection indicatorThe dark pill slides to the room, colour or size you chose.Confirms your choice and links it to the price, stock and image that change with it.200ms
Gallery slideImages slide on swipe, arrows, dots or a colour change.Shows where you are in the set and which image belongs to which colour.320ms
Button states“Add to bag” and “Place order” show a spinner while waiting. Add to bag then draws a check and becomes “View bag”.Confirms the tap worked and that the request is in progress. Stops double taps.200ms label, 320ms check, 700ms per spinner turn
Bag count bumpThe number on the bag icon grows briefly when it changes.Ties the action to where the item went. Screen readers hear the new count.200ms
Bag sheetThe bag slides up over the page, and the page behind dims.Shows the bag is a layer on top of the page, which stays where you left it.320ms open, 200ms close
Rolling quantityThe number rolls up when you add one and down when you remove one.Shows the direction of the change, not only the new number.120ms
Free-delivery barThe bar fills toward ₹2,999 as the bag changes.Shows how far you are from free delivery, as text and as progress.320ms
Promo error shakeThe promo field shakes once next to the error message.Points to the field that needs fixing. The message and icon carry the meaning.320ms
Order confirmedA check draws, then the status timeline fades in step by step.Confirms the order went through and shows what happens next.320ms check, 200ms per step

Not animated on purpose: page changes, toasts, skeleton loaders, and colour changes on hover and press. They work better as instant changes.
