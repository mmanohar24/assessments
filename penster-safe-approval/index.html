About this concept

Safe approval, in minutes

A working prototype of how a doctor could approve a week of posts quickly, with a plain-words safety check before anything goes live. Made by Manoj Venkatesun Manoharan as an independent concept. Not affiliated with Penster. Smile Studio Dental and Dr. Meera Rao are made up.

Try this (about 3 minutes)

  1. Tap Log in and type any number. See what happens when there is no account.
  2. Create the clinic account. It is 3 short steps.
  3. Approve the first post and watch the signup timer stop.
  4. Open Whitening offer and fix the flagged words with one tap.
  5. Go back and tap Approve all safe to see what gets held back, and why.

Why this matters

A clinic is responsible for what it posts. If the check explains itself in plain words and offers a safer line, a busy doctor can approve with confidence instead of avoiding the queue.
